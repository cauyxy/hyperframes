## Drift controls

Before editing any route:

1. read the complete current page;
2. name its journey and page type;
3. verify claims against current source or real behavior;
4. add the redirect before removing or renaming a route;
5. review the rendered page at desktop and 390px mobile;
6. classify every visual and video by its actual job;
7. record remaining risk in `NOTES.md`.

After every major section, provide a browser preview, exact changed and
redirected routes, and an honest list of anything not yet verified.
